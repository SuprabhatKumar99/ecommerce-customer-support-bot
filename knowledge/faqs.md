# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How do I track my order?
You can track your order at any time by asking our virtual assistant or entering your Order ID. You will receive real-time carrier tracking details and estimated delivery dates.

### Can I change my delivery address?
Delivery addresses can only be modified if your order is still in `PENDING` status. Once an order transitions to `PROCESSING` or `SHIPPED`, address modifications can no longer be processed automatically and require immediate human agent escalation.

### How do I contact customer support?
Our automated assistant is available 24/7. If your query requires specialized human assistance, you can ask for a human agent or request escalation, which creates an agent ticket with complete conversation context.

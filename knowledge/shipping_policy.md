# Shipping & Delivery Policy

### Delivery Timeframes
- Standard Ground Shipping: 3 to 5 business days across continental regions.
- Expedited Priority Shipping: 1 to 2 business days.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST ship the next business day.

### Carrier Partnerships
We ship exclusively via FedEx, UPS, and USPS. Real-time tracking numbers are updated automatically once packages leave the warehouse.

### Delayed or Lost Shipments
If your order has not arrived within 48 hours past the estimated delivery date, our support system will automatically flag the delivery issue for human support investigation and claim filing.
